## faq-housing-001

### Question

What kind of room will I get as a first-year student?

### Answer

Most first-year students share a double room with another first-year student. A limited number of single and triple rooms are also available. First-year students are part of the First-Year Residential Experience (FYRE).

### Escalation

Contact your area office if you have questions about your specific room assignment.

## faq-housing-008

### Question

How do I change rooms if there is a problem with my roommate?

### Answer

All first-year students complete a Shared Living Agreement with their roommate. A room change is possible after mediation with the roommate(s) and reaching out to your Resident Advisors and graduate head residents. Because of high new-student load, relocation may not always be available.

### Escalation

Contact your Resident Advisor or graduate head resident to begin a room-change request.

## faq-housing-010

### Question

How do I get into my residence hall and room?

### Answer

All access to student rooms is kept locked. Each student is issued a University ID Card that activates the doors in their hall, plus physical room-door keys. You must present a valid UR ID Card when asked by a University official, including student staff.

### Escalation

Contact your area office if your ID card does not open your hall doors.
