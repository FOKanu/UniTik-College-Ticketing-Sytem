## faq-registrar-009

### Question

How much does a transcript cost?

### Answer

## faq-registrar-022

### Question

What is an apostille request and how do I start one?

### Answer

An apostille request is made when you need a certified transcript and/or diploma as proof for an international institution that UR is legally authorized to grant degrees. The certifying statement UR completes is only the first step. To start: complete the Apostille Request Form and email it to registrar@rochester.edu; UR then prepares a notarized certifying statement, which you submit to the Monroe County Clerk's Office and then to the New York State Department of State.

### Escalation

Contact the Registrar at registrar@rochester.edu for questions during the apostille process.
